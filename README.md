# Philippines_PSMCs
Identifying shared patterns of historical demography across Philippine fishes using Pairwise Sequentially Markovian Coalescent (PSMC) analyses.

PSMC analyses were performed on genomes generated by the Philippines PIRE project for >20 species of marine fish, with essential aid from Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) students attending the 2022 workshop at Silliman University.

Subsequent analyses were performed in R (minmax_latlong_calc.R) to extract estimated population sizes at different times across species from PSMC results and to correlate these with species characteristics.

Some preliminary results were presented at the Western Society of Naturalists meeting in 2023:
